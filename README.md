# Worst Superpower

A one-line entry for the make.fun "Worst Superpower" bounty: invent the most useless superpower in a single line of at most 140 characters.

## Entry

> I can freeze time, but only while I am asleep — the whole world holds its breath all night, then exhales the second I open my eyes.

The power is genuinely useless — it can only ever run while its owner is unconscious, so nothing is ever gained from it — and the image of the whole world holding its breath all night, then exhaling the moment the hero wakes, is the joke.
